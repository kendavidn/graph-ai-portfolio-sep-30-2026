01 / Background

About Me

I am a PhD candidate in epidemiology at the University of Geneva (Infectious Diseases & Mathematical Modelling Group), trained in data science and web development. Expert in R, and proficient in Python and JavaScript.

My research focuses on survival and treatment outcomes among HIV-positive populations, routine surveillance analyses on the Swiss Influenza patient database (CH-SUR), and public-facing epidemiological reporting.

I am also co-founder and co-director of The GRAPH Courses, an open-access platform delivering project-based health data science bootcamps in partnership with universities and international organizations including WHO and the University of Geneva.

02 / The Curriculum

About the Course

Based at the University of Geneva, combining data science fundamentals with practical, responsible task automation for real-world research workflows.

01

Live Workshops & Practice

Weekly live sessions engaging in data analysis code-alongs in RStudio, peer discussions, and hands-on exercises to master prompt patterns.

02

Responsible AI & Verification

Addressing the verification problem, managing non-determinism and context windows, spotting statistical hallucinations, and avoiding "AI slop".

03

Workflow Automation

Building custom RAG chatbots, batch-calling LLM APIs for structured literature review, and trigger-based research notification pipelines.

8-Week Cohort Learning Structure

Organized around weekly prework tasks (~4 hours), theory quizzes, interactive instructor demos, and applied group projects.

Duration 8 Weeks Live
